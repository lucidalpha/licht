  • NASA Astrobiology: "Autocatalytic Reactions and the Search for Life", 2023.

  • Frontiers in Chemistry: "Native Chemical Computation. A Generic Application of Oscillating Chemistry Illustrated With the Belousov-Zhabotinsky Reaction. A Review", 2021.
  • Dennis Bray: "Protein molecules as computational elements in living cells", Nature, 1995.
  • Lulu Qian, Erik Winfree und Jehoshua Bruck: "Neural network computation with DNA strand displacement cascades", Nature, 2011.
  • David Y. Zhang und Georg Seelig: "Dynamic DNA nanotechnology using strand-displacement reactions", Nature Chemistry, 2011.
  • Brian P. Teague, Lulu Qian und Erik Winfree: "Programming and training rate-independent chemical reaction networks", Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 2022.
  • Ilya Prigogine: "Time, Structure and Fluctuations", Nobel Lecture, 1977.
  • Stuart A. Kauffman: "Autocatalytic sets of proteins", Journal of Theoretical Biology, 1986.
  • François Jacob und Jacques Monod: "Genetic Regulatory Mechanisms in the Synthesis of Proteins", Journal of Molecular Biology, 1961.
  • James E. Ferrell und Sang Hoon Ha: "Ultrasensitivity part III: cascades, bistable switches, and oscillators", Trends in Biochemical Sciences, 2014.
  • Mikhail N. Levit und Jeffry B. Stock: "Receptor Methylation Controls the Magnitude of Stimulus-Response Coupling in Bacterial Chemotaxis", Journal of Biological Chemistry, 2002.
  • Harold S. Bernhardt: "The RNA world hypothesis: the worst theory of the early evolution of life except for all the others", Biology Direct, 2012.
  • Uri Alon: "An Introduction to Systems Biology: Design Principles of Biological Circuits", Chapman and Hall/CRC, 2007.
  • John J. Tyson, Katherine C. Chen und Bela Novak: "Sniffers, buzzers, toggles and blinkers: dynamics of regulatory and signaling pathways in the cell", Current Opinion in Cell Biology, 2003.
  • Georg Seelig, David Soloveichik, David Y. Zhang und Erik Winfree: "Enzyme-free nucleic acid logic circuits", Science, 2006.
